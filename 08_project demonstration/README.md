# PROJECT DEMONSTRATION

## Auto Ticket Classification Using Flow Designer

The project demonstration shows how **ServiceNow Flow Designer** can be used to automate the classification and routing of support tickets. The demonstration begins with the creation of a new ticket containing information such as the issue description, category, urgency, and other relevant details.

Once the ticket is submitted, the configured **Flow Designer workflow is automatically triggered**. The flow checks the ticket information and evaluates it against predefined conditions. Based on the identified issue, the system automatically determines the appropriate **ticket category, priority, and assignment group**.

After classification, the flow updates the ticket record and assigns it to the relevant support team. The system can also generate notifications or perform additional actions according to the workflow conditions. The complete process can be monitored through the ServiceNow platform.

### Demonstration Steps

1. **Create a Ticket** – Enter the required ticket details and submit the request.
2. **Trigger the Flow** – The Flow Designer automatically starts when the ticket is created.
3. **Analyze Ticket Details** – The workflow evaluates the information provided in the ticket.
4. **Classify the Ticket** – The ticket is assigned to the appropriate category and subcategory.
5. **Set Priority** – The workflow determines the priority based on predefined conditions.
6. **Assign Support Group** – The ticket is automatically routed to the appropriate support team.
7. **Update Ticket** – The relevant fields and status are updated automatically.
8. **Send Notification** – Notifications can be triggered to inform the responsible team or users.
9. **Verify the Result** – The final ticket record is checked to confirm that classification and assignment were completed correctly.

### Demonstration Outcome

The demonstration shows that the ticket classification process can be performed automatically with minimal manual intervention. The Flow Designer workflow provides a structured process for **classification, prioritization, assignment, and notification**, helping to make ticket management faster, consistent, and easier to maintain.

**Overall Flow:**

**New Ticket → Flow Trigger → Analyze Details → Classify → Set Priority → Assign Group → Update Ticket → Notification → Resolution Process**
